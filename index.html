About

I am a Ph.D. student at School of Computing and Data Science, The University of Hong Kong, where I work with Prof. Yiming Qiu. My research focuses on networking systems and agentic systems.

I received my M.S. degree from Shanghai Jiao Tong University, advised by Prof. Yibo Pi. Previously, I received my B.S. degree from Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications.
